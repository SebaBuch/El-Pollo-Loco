Difficulty

Easy: the normal game.
Impossible: expect the unexpected.

How to play

Help Pepe cross the desert and defeat the giant chicken.

  • Arrow left / right: Walk
  • Space: Jump
  • D: Throw bottle
  • M: Mute / unmute

Jump on chickens to defeat them. Collect bottles to fight the boss and grab coins on your way.
